Why we ask so little

Most risk questionnaires ask twenty hypothetical questions and produce a label. Yours answers itself: twelve years of real decisions, including one real crash, are better evidence than any hypothetical.

We ask only for the things behaviour cannot reveal — what you will not own, and how much you want to hear from us.
