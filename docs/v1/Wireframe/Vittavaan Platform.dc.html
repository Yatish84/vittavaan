Read this before the numbers

Nobody's full portfolio is public in India. What is public: shareholding above 1% in listed companies, bulk and block deals, insider trading disclosures, promoter pledges, and fund houses' monthly holdings. That is a fraction of any real balance sheet — no property, no private companies, no debt. Treating these as complete portfolios would be the most misleading thing this product could do, so each card says what it excludes.
