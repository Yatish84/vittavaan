What a rating means here
✓Only clients who paid through a Vittavaan engagement can rate

✓Credentials are checked against the registering body, with the date
—A professional cannot remove or reply to a rating

A 4.9 from 141 engagements and a 4.9 from three are not the same claim, so we never let them look alike.

How an engagement works
01You state the question. The fee is fixed and shown before you commit.
02You choose exactly what they can read — a scope, not your whole ledger.
03Their answer lands in your data room as a dated document you keep.
04Access ends when the engagement does, and every read is logged.

The advice is theirs and the liability is theirs. We are the record, the scope and the payment rail — not a party to it.

Where this needs counsel

Running a rated panel and taking the payment may make us more than a listing service. Whether Vittavaan can operate this, and under what registration, is a question for counsel before the first engagement is taken.
