Where anonymity gets hard

Enough true characteristics can identify one person even without a name. A 40–44 year old in tier-1 west India with ₹5.94 Cr verified and a specific guarantee is close to unique. Before this ships, the profile needs a re-identification test — coarsen bands until no profile matches fewer than k real borrowers, and refuse to publish when it does.

Before any of this is built

Both need counsel: what Vittavaan may operate, under whose licence, and what the empanelment contract has to forbid.
