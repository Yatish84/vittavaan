The product in one sentence

Know what your household owns, well enough that other people will act on it.

Every capability follows from that. Analysis is the ledger explained. Planning is the ledger projected. Credit and the marketplace are the ledger accepted by a third party. The resume is the ledger shown to someone. Succession is the ledger surviving you. Nothing works if the first clause is not true, which is why verification is the spine of the whole system rather than a badge on a profile.

1 · The spine — how a fact earns its way up

Every number in Vittavaan sits at one of four levels. What a user is allowed to do with a number depends only on its level, and the same rule applies on every screen.

Level 1
Self-declared
You typed it. The stamp collection at ₹50,00,000, the gold in a locker, a startup mark on paper.
Shows in your net worth. Excluded from every figure anyone else relies on.
Level 2
Documented
A registered deed, a contract, a receipt. True as a fact, but its current value is still an opinion.
Provable ownership. Not yet a number a lender will price.
Level 3
Verified
An account aggregator feed, a depository holding, a CA attestation, a registered valuer's report.
Counts toward credit headroom, the resume and the blackbox profile. Carries an as-of date and goes stale.
Level 4
Conduct
CIBIL and CRIF. Not what you own — how you have behaved with other people's money.
The half of the picture assets cannot show, and the half lenders price hardest.

The Rao household is 81% verified by value, and the 6% is the interesting part: a stamp collection recorded at cost, a startup mark that has never been realised, and gold nobody has weighed. Ananya's ₹6,47,00,000 becomes ₹5,26,00,000 the moment a lender looks at it, and the platform's job is to say that before the lender does.

No single feature did that. The continuity did.

4 · The same six steps, every time value changes hands

Credit, a stamp collection, a plot of land, a gift, an estate. Five very different transactions that a user learns once, because they are the same shape.

4 · CommitChoose one bid, consent to PII releaseAgreement, with the rejection clauseRegistered, both presentTransmission, five institutions

Step 2 is where the product earns its money and step 6 is where it earns its next five years. Most platforms do 3, 4 and 5 and leave the user to discover afterwards what they actually bought and what it will cost them in a decade.

6 · Eight rules that hold on every screen

01
Every number carries where it came from and when
A figure with no as-of date and no source is not a figure, it is a claim. This is why the household reads 81% verified rather than "₹6.47 Cr".
02
Say the uncomfortable number in the same breath
14.2%, and 12.4% without the unrealised lines. ₹50,00,000 agreed, and ₹51,10,500 to complete. The second number is the honest one.
03
Name what was not checked
Five sealed stamp plates. Market value, "not our role, never." An unstated gap is read as a guarantee.
04
Identity is released once, deliberately, to one party
Nine lenders bid without a name. One learns it, at a moment the user chose, on terms shown on screen, with a log.
05
Household visibility is granted, never assumed
Ananya administers her father's account and cannot see his will. Nirmala is notified of the gift, not asked to consent to it.
06
The platform never moves the money
The user authorises inside their own bank. We read balances and hold no float. Escrow is a licensed bank's job, or the registry's.
07
Judgement is bought from a named professional
Advocates, valuers, CAs. Their opinion is theirs, addressed to the user, dated, and stored. We supply the brief, not the view.
08
Nothing is deleted, and the record outlives the account
A superseded will is kept, marked superseded. A dead man's account stays readable. In 2071 the chain from a 1996 title search is one file.

Notifications and prompts as a system
Blocked on counsel
Running a bidding contest between lenders is likely a regulated arrangement, not a listing service. The anonymised profile is personal data under the DPDP Act and needs a k-anonymity test before publishing. Escrow with an embedded payment link and a completion fee approaches payment-aggregator and nodal-account rules. Ask, life-event prompts and any recommendation sit near the advice boundary.
None of these are design problems. All of them decide what ships.
