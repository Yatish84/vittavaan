Four things this page cannot tell you
—Whether the property valuations are right. 38% of your assets are marked to an estimate you entered, not to a sale.
—What you would have earned had you invested the ₹31,20,000 of fresh money at a different moment. Returns here are time-weighted; your own experience was money-weighted.
—Anything about tax. A 15.9% gain you have not realised is worth more than one you have.
—EPF and NPS, which are not linked. They would sit in a seventh row and would lower your equity weight.
The uncomfortable line

Over three years your equity earned 19.6% a year and your land earned 7.8%. Land is 42% of the portfolio. You did not choose that allocation — it arrived through two flats bought to live in, and it will decide most of your return for the next decade whatever you pick inside the equity sleeve.
