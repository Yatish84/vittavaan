These are fictional investors

Every name, figure and holding on this tab is invented, to show what the directory would look like. Attaching made-up portfolio numbers to a real, named investor would be a false statement about that person's finances, so the design uses archetypes drawn from the kinds of people you named — a global macro manager, a value investor, a studio producer, a cricketer, a sitting chief executive — rather than the people themselves.

In a live product each of these cards would be claimed and published by the person it belongs to, under their own consent, with the platform verifying only what it can actually see.

What a verification badge means
LedgerRead directly from a linked account. The platform saw it.
FiledMatched against an exchange or registrar filing.
AttestedA named professional signed for it. Not independently checked.
ClaimedThey typed it. Treat exactly as you would a tweet.

Your own profile carries the same four badges, field by field. 81% of your ledger is Ledger-verified; the property estimates and the crypto are Claimed.

Distance is a setting

A public investor can publish to millions and still accept questions from nobody. Reach and access are separate switches here: one controls who can read, the other who can write. Most of these nine read-only.

There is no reply box, no quote, no reshare. What the medium allows is following, and asking a question the author may answer publicly once a quarter.

Curation has a limit

Choosing which true things to show is ordinary and fair. Choosing which true things to hide so a reader draws a false conclusion is not, and in a lender's case it is actionable. The grid will let you build the first and refuses to build the second.

Rules the ledger enforces
01No grant is permanent by default. Standing grants are re-confirmed yearly.
02No grant can be given in exchange for another.
03A professional's grant is scoped to the work and lapses when the work ends.
04A reader cannot export or forward what they are shown, and is told so.
05Nothing disclosed here reaches the Marketplace, a lender's pricing engine, or an advertiser.
06An ignored request expires in thirty days rather than escalating.
