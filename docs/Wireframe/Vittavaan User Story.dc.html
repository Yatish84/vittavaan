The rule under everything
Every stored value has a source, an as-of timestamp and a verification level. What a user may do with a value is a function of its level, enforced server-side, never in the view.

Act one · Getting the ledger true

Scene 00She links the accounts
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Pages · Linked accounts & consent
The story

Ananya has spreadsheets going back to 2014 and no confidence in them. She connects banks and a depository through an account aggregator, uploads the commercial property papers, and types in the things nothing can fetch — the Sangli land, a startup stake, gold.

The screen does not congratulate her on being connected. It tells her what proportion of her wealth is now provable, and what is not.

What the system does
Consent is per-institution, time-boxed and revocable, with the expiry visible and a renewal prompt before it lapses.
Each fetched holding is written with source, fetch timestamp and level 3. Manual entries land at level 1.
Uploaded documents are OCR'd for dates and amounts but never auto-promote a value's level. A document takes a holding to level 2, no further.
Verified share is computed by value, not by count, and recomputed on every write.

Nothing linked yet — the true empty state, and the most important screen in the product
Fetch failed, stale data shown with its age
Consent expiring in 14 days
Consent revoked, history retained
Duplicate holding detected across two feeds
Scene 01The first honest look
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Dashboard v3
The story

₹6,47,00,000, and immediately underneath it the sentence that matters: 94% of it is verified. She has never seen the other 6% named before.

This is the home screen, and it is the only screen most users will see most days. It answers one question completely rather than five questions partially.

What the system does
Net worth is assets minus liabilities across every holder the viewer has rights over, priced at the last close, stamped with that time.
No figure renders without its as-of. If a price is older than its class's staleness tolerance, it renders marked stale rather than fresh.
Movement is shown against a stated period and never annualised on a home screen.
No marketplace or product surface may appear here. Enforced as a lint rule, not a convention.

A feed down — partial figure, disclosed

Scene 02She goes looking for the detail
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Pages · seven screens
The story

Net worth, then assets, then one holding. Liabilities with their real rates. Cash flow, where she finds that four passive streams cover 61% of the household's fixed outgo. The data room, where eight years of paper finally has an index.

Every one of these is a list that drills to a record. The pattern is worth building once, properly.

What the system does
One holding record serves every view. Class, holder, quantity, cost, current value, valuation basis, level, as-of, linked documents, linked goals, pledge status.
A holding pledged against a facility renders as pledged everywhere it appears, without exception.
Liabilities carry rate, type (fixed or floating), reset date, outstanding, schedule and the asset securing them.
Data room documents are attached to an entity, never to a folder. The folder view is derived.

Scene 03She adds the family
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Household
The story

Six people, four of whom own something. Manoj's ESOPs, the children's guardian folios and Sukanya account, her parents' deposits. The picture that emerges is not the total — it is that 52% of the household sits with one person, and the two people with the least are the two who cannot earn more.

This screen is where the permission model becomes visible, and it is the hardest thing in the product to get right.

What the system does
Four roles: owner, administrator, viewer, dependant. Every asset belongs to exactly one member; the household total is derived, never stored.
An administrator can gather, upload and run processes. Only an owner can commit that owner's money — enforced in the API, with the acting role written into every audit row.
Access is granted by the owner, per member, per asset class, and is revocable. Nothing is visible by default because two people are related.
A minor's assets are administered until majority, then a transfer of control is scheduled and prompted.

Act two · Making sense of it

Scene 04"Has any of this actually worked?"
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Platform · Understand
The story

14.2% money-weighted over five years, against 12.8% time-weighted and a 10.9% benchmark. Then the line that changes how she reads it: two rows of that table are unrealised and self-declared, and without them the number is 12.4%.

She also learns one flexi-cap scheme costs her ₹2,01,600 a year against a category median of 1.10%. There is no button to switch it.

What the system does
XIRR needs every dated cash flow per holding. Where history is incomplete the metric is withheld, never estimated.
Attribution is contribution to total return — average weight times class return — and must sum to the headline. Label it so nobody reads it as class performance.
Every metric is computed twice: with and without level-1 holdings. Both are shown.
Benchmark data is licensed. Confirm terms before the composite index goes anywhere near a screen.

Under 12 months of history — no XIRR
A holding with no benchmark
A class held for part of the period only
Negative XIRR
Costs unavailable for a class
Scene 05Six goals, and the one that does not work
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Platform · Plan
The story

She names six things she is saving for and attaches real holdings to each. Four are on track. One depends on a syndicate exit that has no window. And Priyanka's education needs money in 2037 from an account that matures in 2040.

Nobody had noticed. It took no cleverness — just the maturity date and the target year on the same screen.

What the system does
A goal links to specific holdings, not to an amount. Selling a linked holding must warn which goals it defunds.
Projection is deterministic compounding of funded value plus recorded contributions, at assumptions displayed on the same screen and stored with the goal. Never a Monte Carlo the user cannot interrogate.
Liquidity check per goal: compare each funding holding's availability date against the goal date, and flag the mismatch. This is the rule that caught Priyanka's SSY.
Life-event prompts come only from dated facts already in the ledger — lease end, vesting window, policy renewal, a child's birthday. No inferred events.

Goal funded by an illiquid asset
Goal already met
Goal in the past, unclosed

Assumptions changed — old projection retained
Scene 06Prepay or invest, worked all the way through
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Platform · Calculators
The story

The calculator opens already filled with her ₹1.92 Cr at 8.40%. Eleven years out, the two paths are ₹3,50,000 apart — under half a percent a year — and the crossover is 9.6%.

The answer is that it barely matters, which no calculator has ever told her before. She saves it into the goal, assumptions attached.

What the system does
Every calculator seeds from the user's own holdings and liabilities. A blank calculator is a failure state.
Every input is visible and editable. No hidden constant anywhere in the engine.
A saved result stores its inputs, assumptions and the date, so the user can see in 2029 what they believed in 2026.
Every calculator states what it excludes. Tax deductions, prepayment charges, the value of being debt-free.

Scene 07She just asks
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Platform · Ask
The story

"What did we spend on the Bengaluru flat over three years, and what has it returned including rent?" No dashboard anticipates that question.

It answers in a table, tags every source, flags that two thirds of the return is her own estimate rather than a market price, and tells her February 2025 is missing because the flat was empty — excluded, not averaged.

What the system does
Retrieval over the user's own ledger plus a whitelist of named public sources. No open-web answers, ever.
Arithmetic is done by the same engines the screens use, not by the model. The model composes; it does not compute.
Every figure inherits its as-of and level. Level-1 inputs are called out inside the answer.
Four hard refusals: no buy/sell/hold, no prediction, no source outside the whitelist, no gap filled with an average. On a gap, say so and offer to link what is missing.
States to build
Ledger cannot answer
Partially answerable — answer the part
Question asks for advice — refuse and offer a professional
Answer spans members the asker cannot see
Every answer logged and replayable

Act three · Other people act on it

Scene 08Proving it without showing it
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Financial resume
The story

A syndicate lead wants to know she can write the cheque. A landlord wants comfort. Neither should learn her net worth.

She sends a resume of verified claims — "investor since 2014", "operating income above ₹50 L, verified", "96 of 96 instalments on time", "3 exits" — chooses each field, sets an expiry, and can see afterwards who opened it.

What the system does
Only level-3 and level-4 facts are publishable. A self-declared figure can never appear on a resume, in any form.
Claims are bands, counts, durations and booleans. No exact amount is ever emitted, and bands are wide enough that a share cannot be reversed into a balance.
A share is a signed, expiring link to a specific recipient, revocable at any moment, with every open logged and shown to the owner.
Claims re-derive on view. A revoked consent or a lapsed attestation collapses the claim rather than serving a stale one.
States to build
Owner view versus recipient view — genuinely different renders
Claim expired mid-view
Link revoked after opening
Recipient forwards the link — must fail
Nothing verified yet — empty resume
Scene 09₹50,00,000, and nine lenders who do not know her name
Aug 2026 · Ananya
Credit · six steps
The story

She registers a need, not an application. Her financial characteristics go out anonymised to 34 empanelled lenders. Nine bid over 72 hours, from 8.45% to 11.20%.

She picks one. Only then does she sign a consent releasing her identity — to that lender alone. The other eight are told the profile closed, and nothing else.

What the system does
The profile is built only from level-3 wealth and level-4 conduct. Self-declared holdings are excluded and the exclusion is shown to the user.
Before publishing, run a re-identification test. Coarsen bands until the profile matches at least k real borrowers; refuse to publish when it cannot. Holdings by class and value only — never by security name.
Bids are structured objects, not documents: APR, EMI, total cost, fees, LTV trigger, cure period, prepayment terms. Comparison is impossible otherwise.
PII release is a discrete signed event, scoped to one lender, one purpose, with a deletion clock and a revoke path. Repricing after release is logged against the lender and shown on their future bids.
States to build
No bids at all
One bid only — no comparison to make
Profile fails the k-test
User takes none and lets it expire
Lender asks a question — user approves the answer
Lender reprices after release
Scene 10The stamps — buying something nobody can price
Sep 2026 · Ananya
Marketplace · six steps
The story

A 24-plate pre-independence collection. She reads what was verified and, more usefully, what was not — five plates sealed since 1994, and no opinion at all on value.

Eleven messages, ₹58 L down to ₹50 L, escrow, handover at the vault with the expert present. It goes onto her books at cost, self-declared, and drops her verified share.

What the system does
Verification is a list of performed checks, each with who, when and outcome — plus an explicit not-checked list. Never a badge, never a score.
The platform emits no valuation for any listed asset. This is a hard product rule with legal weight.
Escrow is a licensed bank account. Payment is authorised inside the buyer's own bank; we hold no float and cannot initiate a transfer.
On completion the asset, its cost, the agreement, the thread and every document copy into both parties' data rooms.

Scene 11The plot — the same six steps, a different legal world
Sep 2026 · Vasant, Ananya administering
Marketplace · land
The story

Her father wants land in Karjat. She runs the process; he authorises the money. A title search, then a site visit that finds three things the listing did not say — unfinished approach road, no drain, and the "borewell" is on the neighbour's plot.

₹38 L becomes ₹36.20 L. Payment and registration happen in the same room; the registry is the escrow.

What the system does
Asset class drives the flow. Land adds title search, encumbrance certificate, site visit, public notice and registration; the six-step spine is unchanged.
Comparables come from registered sub-registrar records only. Asking prices are never shown as evidence of value.
Administrator runs every step; the owner's authentication is required at price agreement, at signing and at payment. Each is logged against him, not her.
Mutation is tracked as an open item after completion, with a prompt at four weeks. Registration is not the end of the transaction.

Act four · It passes on

Scene 12Her father gives her the plot
Sep 2027 · Vasant
Gift transfer · five steps
The story

A transfer between two people already on the platform. No buyer, no escrow, and no money moving except duty to the state.

₹48,100 all in because the advocate confirmed the family concession in writing a week early. The plot arrives in Ananya's ledger carrying his cost base and his 2026 clock.

What the system does
An intra-household transfer moves the asset between members and preserves cost base and acquisition date. This is the single most valuable thing the ledger carries, and losing it is the most expensive bug in the product.
Only the owner can initiate a transfer out of their own assets. An administrator cannot, however broad their access.
Other affected members are notified, not asked to consent, unless they have a legal interest. Notification fires at draft, not after registration.
Completion re-runs succession checks on both members and raises a prompt where a will no longer matches the assets.

Donee is not on the platform
Asset is pledged — must block

Scene 13The gift breaks his will
Nov 2027 · Vasant
Will · five steps
The story

His 2011 will leaves "all immovable property" to Ananya. He has none. Every clause is empty, there is no residuary clause, and the executor died in 2021 — so his entire ₹37,70,000 would pass by intestacy.

The platform assembles a twelve-page brief, records his answers in his own words, and hands both to an advocate. It writes nothing.

What the system does
A will is a record with a date, a location, an executor and a coverage state — never a document we generate or hold as the original.
Coverage is computed: every asset the will names against every asset the ledger holds. Uncovered value is the headline number.
Nomination-versus-will conflicts are surfaced per account. A dead nominee is an error state, not a warning.
Succession answers are visible to the owner and their chosen professional only. An administrator with full asset access still cannot read them, and the household view must not reveal that the record exists.
States to build
No will at all
Will exists, location unknown
Executor deceased
Beneficiary deceased
Two wills, unclear which governs
Superseded will retained and marked
Scene 14Her father dies, and the ledger does its last job
Mar–Aug 2031 · Ananya as executor
Inheritance · five steps
The story

The product's first act is to go quiet. His account freezes read-only, every prompt to him is suspended, and the screen says nothing here is urgent for thirty days.

Four months and twenty-six days later the estate is closed, with no succession certificate anywhere — because of two nomination fixes made in 2027 that cost nothing.

What the system does
A death event freezes the member's account to read-only, suspends all notifications to them, and opens an estate with a date-of-death valuation snapshot that never moves again.
Executor authority is a distinct role derived from the will, separate from any prior administrator access, and every action records which authority was used.
Income apportions at the date of death across two returns. Transmitted assets carry cost base and holding period to the beneficiary — the same rule as a gift.
Prompts arising from a death are dated and queued, and surface when the affected person next opens their own account. Never pushed, never routed via a relative.

No will — intestacy shares computed and labelled as default
No nominee — succession certificate path
Claim arrives after distribution
Executor declines — alternate steps in
Estate is insolvent
Beneficiary predeceased
Account closed but readable indefinitely

Cross-cutting · true on every screen

If a scene above contradicts one of these, the scene is wrong.

Verification level gates capability, server-side
Credit headroom, resume claims and blackbox profiles read level 3 and 4 only. A client that forgets is a bug; a server that allows it is a breach.
Every figure ships with as-of and source
Make it impossible to render a number without them — a formatter that takes a bare value should not exist in the codebase.
Staleness is per class, and visible
Listed equity goes stale in a day, property in two years, a collectible in two. Past tolerance, the figure renders marked and is excluded from verified totals.
The platform never holds or moves money
Read-only bank access. Payment is authorised in the user's own bank; escrow is a licensed bank's account. No float, ever.
Six-step transaction spine, one implementation
Intent, verify, negotiate, commit, settle, record. Asset class supplies the checks and the settlement mechanism; the state machine is shared.
Analysis surfaces carry no commercial call to action
Wealth, Understand and Plan cannot link to a paid action. Market states its fee in the header of every screen. Enforce in CI.
Documents attach to entities and are immutable
Superseded, never overwritten or deleted. A document copied to a counterparty's data room is a copy with its own access record.
Every read of another person's data is logged and shown
Administrator views, lender reads, resume opens. The log is the owner's to see, and it is a product surface, not an internal table.
