Why land is different

A stamp collection is either genuine or not. Land can have a perfect chain of title and still carry a tenancy from 1974, a reservation in a plan nobody reads, or a developer who never built the road. Nine checks, and the two that remain open are the ones that cost money later.

Why no escrow here

The stamp collection needed escrow because handover and payment were days apart. Land registration is simultaneous — the deed is executed only once the seller confirms the credit, and the sub-registrar records the UTR on the document. The registry itself is the escrow.
